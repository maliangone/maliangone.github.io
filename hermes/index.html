hermes

A personal automation tool.

hermes is a private, single-user automation assistant. It runs on its owner's own hardware and connects to the owner's own accounts — Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Gmail and Outlook/Hotmail — to help organise files, keep a calendar, and draft or send email on the owner's behalf.

It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it offers no third-party access to anyone else's data.

See the privacy policy for details on what it accesses and how that data is handled.
