Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

hermes is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by its owner for their own accounts. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive / Docs / Sheets — to find, create, upload and organise the owner's own files.
  • Google Calendar — to read and manage the owner's own calendar events.
  • Gmail — to read, search, label and send email from the owner's own account.
  • Microsoft (Outlook / Hotmail) — to read and send email from the owner's own mailbox.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests, such as summarising email, preparing files, or scheduling events. It is processed locally on the owner's own machine.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or profiling.
  • No data is transferred to any external server other than the APIs of the services listed above, which are needed to perform the requested task.
  • The tool is not made available to any other user.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's own device with restricted file permissions. Cached content is temporary and can be deleted at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from the Microsoft account privacy dashboard. Revoking access stops all further use immediately.
